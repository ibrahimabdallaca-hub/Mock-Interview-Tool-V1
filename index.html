Your questions are built around your job title, difficulty level, and (if you provide them) your employer name and job description, cycling through a broad mix of question categories — situational, technical, behavioural, motivational, and other professional interview question types — with no exact question repeated in one session. This tool uses your camera and microphone only inside this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded anywhere automatically — the video is saved to your own device when you click Download.

Automatic answer scoring uses your browser's built-in speech recognition, which currently works best in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge and requires an internet connection while recording. Your browser may not fully support it — you can still record and download your video either way.

    The overall performance percentage blends your speech-to-text transcript (answer length, pacing, filler words, and coverage of key concepts and job-description terms) with automatic, camera-based estimates of eye contact, posture, gestures, and presentation setup (framing, lighting, background). All of it is an automatic practice indicator, not a certified grade or a real reading of your confidence, emotions, or body language -- the camera-based estimates in particular can be affected by your camera angle, lighting, internet connection, and hardware, and should be treated as rough, encouraging guidance rather than fact.
